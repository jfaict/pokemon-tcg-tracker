# Decision log

> One entry per real choice. Append-only. Never edit past entries — if a decision changes, write a new ADR that supersedes the old one.
> Format inspired by Michael Nygard's ADRs.

## ADR-001 · <short title>
- **Date:**
- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Type:** functional | technical
- **Context:** What is the situation? What forces are in play?
- **Decision:** What did we decide?
- **Assumptions:** What did we take as true without proving?
- **Alternatives considered:** Briefly. Why were they rejected?
- **Consequences:** What becomes easier? What becomes harder?
- **Source:** link to the conversation in `conversations/` where this was hashed out
