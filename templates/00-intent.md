# Intent: <feature or project name>

> One-page document. Written by you (or you + chat LLM). The upstream artifact for everything else.

## Vision
One sentence. What does the world look like when this exists?

## Problem
What is broken / missing / annoying today? Be specific. Use the three triage questions:
1. What were you trying to do when you felt the pain?
2. What is the actual friction, stripped of any proposed fix?
3. What would "solved" feel like, regardless of how it gets built?

## Success criteria
Measurable signals that tell you it worked. Prefer behavioural (e.g. "I open the app twice a week") over vanity (e.g. "1000 users").

## Non-goals
What this is explicitly *not*. Just as important as the goals.

## Open questions
Questions you cannot answer yet. They become the agenda for the next phase.

## Source material
Links to the raw notes, voice memos, brainstorm files in `intake/` that this intent was distilled from.

## Conversation references
Links to the saved chat threads in `conversations/` where you talked the idea through with an LLM or another person.
